# Setting up the church WhatsApp: a step-by-step guide

This guide is for the person setting up the church's WhatsApp number on a phone. No technical knowledge is needed.

When you're done, the church will have one WhatsApp number, **"Amazing Grace"**. The founding pastor's phone holds it, and up to four other admins can use it from their own phones or computers. Staff send birthday wishes, visitor follow-ups and care messages from it using the church admin app (Olive).

Every feature in this guide is **free**.

> WhatsApp sometimes moves menu items between app updates. If a menu path below doesn't match exactly, look for the same name under **Settings → Business tools**.

---

## Before you start

You'll need:

- [ ] A **new SIM card**, registered with the founding pastor's NIN
- [ ] The **pastor's phone** (a dual-SIM phone is fine; the church SIM can be the second SIM)
- [ ] The **church logo** as an image on the phone
- [ ] A **church email address** the church controls (for example the church Gmail), not anyone's personal email
- [ ] The **service times** and **church address**
- [ ] About **45 minutes**

---

## Part 1: The SIM card

1. **Register the SIM in the founding pastor's name,** using their NIN. Only the person the SIM is registered to can get a replacement if it's lost, stolen or damaged. Losing the SIM can mean losing the church's WhatsApp.
2. **Keep the line active.** Networks take back lines that go unused for a long time. Once a month, put a little airtime on it and make a short call or send one SMS. This is on the monthly checklist at the end.
3. **Keep the SIM in the phone.** WhatsApp sends a code to this number whenever the account needs to be checked again.

---

## Part 2: Install WhatsApp Business and register the number

1. On the pastor's phone, install **WhatsApp Business** from the Play Store or App Store. It's the app with a **"B"** in the icon, not regular WhatsApp. The pastor's personal WhatsApp stays as it is; the two apps live side by side.
2. Open WhatsApp Business, agree to the terms, and enter the **church SIM's number** (+234…).
3. Enter the 6-digit code WhatsApp sends by SMS.
4. When asked for the business name, type **Amazing Grace**.
5. For the category, choose **Religious Organization**. If that isn't listed, choose **Other**.

---

## Part 3: The business profile (what people see)

Go to **Settings → Business tools → Business profile** and fill in:

| Field | What to enter |
|---|---|
| Profile photo | The church logo, centred, with nothing important near the edges because the photo is shown as a circle |
| Name | **Amazing Grace** |
| Category | Religious Organization |
| Description | Abiding Word Of Grace Missions (PKA Amazing Grace Bible Church). Salvation, healing and deliverance. Join us this Sunday! |
| Address | The church's full address. This shows a map pin so visitors can find you |
| Business hours | The service times, e.g. Sunday 8:00 am–12:00 pm, plus midweek service |
| Email | info@abwog.org |
| Website | https://abwog.org |

---

## Part 4: Security (do not skip this)

### Two-step verification
This stops anyone from taking over the church account, even if they get hold of the SIM.

1. **Settings → Account → Two-step verification → Turn on.**
2. Choose a **6-digit PIN**. Write it down and keep it somewhere safe that the founding pastor **and one other trusted leader** know about.
3. When asked for an email, enter the **church email**, not a personal one. If the PIN is ever forgotten, this email is used to reset it.

### Chat backups
These keep the message history if the phone is lost or replaced.

- **Android:** Settings → Chats → Chat backup → back up to Google Drive → **Daily**. Use a Google account the church controls if possible.
- **iPhone:** Settings → Chats → Chat backup → Auto backup → **Daily** (iCloud).
- Turn off "Include videos" to save space and data.

---

## Part 5: Linking other admins (up to 4 devices)

The church number lives on the pastor's phone, but **up to 4 other devices** can use it at the same time. Every device sees the same chats.

### On a computer (easiest; recommended for admins who work in Olive)
1. On the computer, go to **web.whatsapp.com**. A QR code appears.
2. On the pastor's phone, open WhatsApp Business → **Settings → Linked devices → Link a device**.
3. Point the phone at the QR code on the computer. Done.

When an admin clicks "Message on WhatsApp" in Olive on that computer, the message opens in this church session.

### On an admin's own phone
The admin keeps their **personal WhatsApp** as it is and installs **WhatsApp Business** separately for the church number.

1. On the admin's phone, install **WhatsApp Business**.
2. Open it. On the first screen, tap the **⋮ / More** menu (or "Link to existing account") and choose **Link as companion device**. A QR code appears.
3. On the pastor's phone: **Settings → Linked devices → Link a device**, then scan the QR code on the admin's phone.

**⚠️ Important for admins with two WhatsApps on one phone:** before sending any church message, **check that you're in WhatsApp Business (the "B" app), not your personal WhatsApp.** Olive opens WhatsApp Business automatically on Android phones. On iPhones it can't choose, so the admin must check.

### Rules for linked devices
- **Maximum of 4 linked devices.** Use them for the admins who send messages most often. Others can share a computer.
- **The pastor's phone must open WhatsApp Business at least once every 14 days.** Otherwise all linked devices are logged out and have to be linked again.
- **Keep a list** of who has which linked device (name, device, date linked).
- **When an admin leaves the team,** remove their device straight away: on the pastor's phone, **Settings → Linked devices**, tap their device, then **Log out**.

## Part 6: Automatic messages (free)

### Greeting message
Sent automatically when someone messages the church for the first time, or after 14 days of no messages.

**Settings → Business tools → Greeting message → On**, recipients: **Everyone**.

> Hello! 👋 Welcome to Amazing Grace (Abiding Word Of Grace Missions). Thank you for reaching out. Someone from our team will reply to you shortly. God bless you!

### Away message
Sent automatically outside the hours you choose.

**Settings → Business tools → Away message → On**, schedule: **Outside of business hours** (uses the hours from Part 3) or a custom schedule.

> Thank you for your message! 🙏 Our team isn't available right now, but we'll reply as soon as we can. For urgent prayer needs, please call +234 XXX XXX XXXX. God bless you!

Replace the number with the pastoral emergency line, or remove that sentence.

## Part 7: Quick replies (saved answers)

These save typing for questions the church gets often. In any chat, type **/** and pick one.

**Settings → Business tools → Quick replies → +**

| Shortcut | Message |
|---|---|
| `/services` | Our services: Sunday Worship 8:00 am. Midweek Service [day & time]. We'd love to see you! 😊 |
| `/address` | We're located at [full church address]. Here's the map: [Google Maps link] |
| `/prayer` | Thank you for sharing this with us. 🙏 We are standing with you in prayer. Is there anything else we can do for you? |
| `/welcome` | It was wonderful to have you at Amazing Grace! We hope to see you again this Sunday. |
| `/profile` | Please help us keep your details up to date: https://app.abwog.org/profile. It only takes 2 minutes! |
| `/thanks` | Thank you so much! God bless you. 🙏 |

## Part 8: Labels (keep conversations organised)

Labels are coloured tags on chats, so the team can see at a glance who needs what.

**Settings → Business tools → Labels**: delete the default shop labels ("New order", "Pending payment" and so on) and create these:

| Label | Use it for |
|---|---|
| 🟢 New visitor | First-time visitors who have messaged or been messaged |
| 🟡 Follow-up | Visitors we're following up with |
| 🔵 Member | Regular members |
| 🟣 Worker | Workers and department members |
| 🎂 Birthday | Birthday wishes sent this week (remove afterwards) |
| 🙏 Prayer request | People who asked for prayer |
| 💛 Pastoral care | Illness, bereavement, family support, people we're caring for |
| 🔴 Needs pastor | Conversations a pastor should personally take over |

**To label a chat:** press and hold it in the chat list, tap the **label icon**, and choose one or more labels.

## Part 9: Spreading the word (free)

### Short link and QR code
**Settings → Business tools → Short link.** Turn on the default message and set it to **"Hello Amazing Grace!"**.
- Share the **link** in church announcements and on social media.
- **Print the QR code** and display it in the foyer next to the "Update your details" QR code.
- When people message the church, they're saving a connection with the number, which helps church messages reach them.

### Status (like Instagram stories)
Post the **weekly service flyer**, event flyers or a Bible verse to **Status**. It's seen by everyone who has saved the church number, and it disappears after 24 hours. It costs nothing and is a gentle weekly reminder.

### WhatsApp Channel (for announcements)
A **Channel** is a one-way announcement feed that anyone can follow. There's no limit on followers, and followers can't see each other's numbers.

1. Go to the **Updates** tab → **+** → **New channel**.
2. Name it **Amazing Grace**, add the logo and the description from Part 3.
3. Share the channel link in church and on the website.

Use the Channel for **announcements to everyone** (service updates, events, devotionals). Don't send announcements as individual messages; that's what gets numbers flagged as spam.

### Broadcast lists (use sparingly)
Broadcast lists send one message to many people individually. **They only reach people who have saved the church number.** Prefer the Channel for announcements, and keep personal messages (birthdays, follow-ups, care) for individual chats sent through Olive.

### Catalog (optional)
The catalog is meant for products, but churches can use it to showcase **departments or ministries** (a photo plus a short description of Choir, Media & Tech, Ushers, Children's Ministry and so on). Visitors can browse ways to get involved. Find it under **Settings → Business tools → Catalog**. Skip this if it adds stress.

## Part 10: House rules for admins

1. **Messages come from the church.** Always say who you are. Olive's messages do this automatically ("I'm Pastor John from Amazing Grace").
2. **Use Olive for birthdays, follow-ups and check-ins,** and tap **"Yes, I sent it"** afterwards so the church record stays up to date.
3. **Reply within 24 hours.** If a conversation needs a pastor, label it 🔴 **Needs pastor**.
4. **Keep it private.** Don't screenshot or forward members' messages outside the team. Prayer requests and care conversations are confidential.
5. **Respect "please stop".** If someone asks not to be messaged, reply kindly, then turn on **"Don't message on WhatsApp"** on their profile in Olive.
6. **Don't send many identical messages at once.** Send personal messages one at a time. Olive already varies the wording.
7. **Never share the two-step verification PIN,** and never accept a request to "verify" the account from anyone.

---

## Monthly checklist (5 minutes)

- [ ] Church SIM topped up and used (a short call or SMS)
- [ ] Pastor's phone opened WhatsApp Business in the last 14 days (if not, linked devices will log out)
- [ ] Linked devices reviewed: remove anyone who no longer needs access
- [ ] Last backup is recent (Settings → Chats → Chat backup)
- [ ] Labels tidied (remove old 🎂 Birthday labels)
- [ ] Business hours and away message still match the service times

---

## After setup: tell the Olive admin

Once the number is working, an Olive admin goes to **Settings → Church Information** and enters:
- **Short name used in messages:** Amazing Grace
- **Church WhatsApp number:** the new number (+234…)

Then click **Test** to confirm it opens the right WhatsApp account. Each admin should also set their **display name** under **Settings → My Profile** (e.g. "Pastor John", "Sis. Grace"), so their messages are signed properly.
